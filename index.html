Solutions

Featured Solution

Proposed Client Solution / Prototype

Target Inventory Exception Assistant

An AI-powered decision-support concept designed around the store-level inventory challenges a large retailer may face.

Independent concept developed as an academic consulting project. Target has not commissioned, reviewed, or endorsed this work.

The Challenge

Strong systems, but a lot left to interpret

Large retailers already have sophisticated inventory and replenishment systems. However, store managers may still need to interpret large amounts of inventory, sales, shipment, and supplier information when determining which exceptions require immediate attention.

Managers may need to understand:

  • Which products are most at risk
  • Why a product is at risk
  • Whether an incoming shipment will arrive in time
  • Whether demand has unexpectedly increased
  • Whether a supplier or shipment delay is causing the issue
  • Which inventory exceptions should be handled first

Our Solution

AI-Powered Inventory Exception Assistant

The assistant acts as a decision-support layer on top of existing inventory and replenishment systems. It turns raw operational signals into a short, prioritized list a manager can act on.

It does not replace Target's existing replenishment systems. It helps people interpret what those systems surface, faster and more consistently.

It helps store managers quickly answer:

1What requires attention?
2Why is it happening?
3How urgent is it?
4What action should be taken?

What the assistant does

  • Identifies products at risk of stocking out
  • Analyzes inventory levels
  • Considers sales velocity
  • Considers incoming shipment timing
  • Detects shipment delays
  • Detects potential demand spikes
  • Ranks inventory exceptions by urgency
  • Explains why an item is at risk
  • Recommends an appropriate next action
  • Generates a Top 5 Items Requiring Attention report

Under the hood

How the solution works

An eight-stage AI workflow that moves from raw data to a clear, prioritized summary for store managers.

  1. Step 1

    Inventory Data

    Store-level inventory, sales velocity, shipment information, safety stock, and related operational data.

  2. Step 2

    Data Validation

    Check that the required information is present and correctly formatted.

  3. Step 3

    Metrics Calculation

    Calculate indicators such as days of supply and potential stockout timing.

  4. Step 4

    Risk Analysis

    Determine whether factors such as low inventory, increased demand, or shipment delays create inventory risk.

  5. Step 5

    Risk Classification

    Assign each item a Critical, High, Medium, or Low priority.

  6. Step 6

    Recommended Action

    Suggest the most appropriate response based on the identified cause.

  7. Step 7

    Priority Ranking

    Rank inventory exceptions based on urgency.

  8. Step 8

    Manager Summary

    Present the most important issues in clear language for store managers.

Built with Dify

The prototype workflow is developed on Dify, an AI workflow development platform, which lets each stage be designed, tested, and refined independently.
